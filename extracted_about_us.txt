
About Us: MSB PHARMACHEM
Quality Products, Global Supply
Welcome to MSB PHARMACHEM
MSB PHARMACHEM is a trusted supplier of Pharmaceutical APIs, Bulk Drugs, Pharma Raw Materials, Excipients, Domestic Chemicals, Solvents and Impurity Standards. From our base in Kukatpally, Hyderabad, we serve pharmaceutical manufacturers, formulation companies and chemical industries across India and international markets.
Our name stands on three promises: Quality, Reliability and Trust. Every enquiry, order and shipment we handle is guided by them.
Who We Are
Hyderabad is one of the world's leading pharmaceutical hubs, and it is where MSB PHARMACHEM was built. Our team brings more than 18 years of experience in supply chain management, inventory planning, procurement and vendor coordination. This lets us do more than supply materials. We plan, source and deliver with the discipline that pharma customers expect.
We work as an extension of our customers' procurement teams. We understand that a delayed or inconsistent batch affects production schedules, so we focus on dependable availability, clear documentation and honest communication.
